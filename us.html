Answered vs missed

On weekdays SupportDDS answered 101 of 261 genuine calls and missed 160: 141 rolled to voicemail and 19 callers hung up before reaching it. A further 10 genuine calls came in on weekends and none were answered. Another 111 calls were spam.

Of the 36 calls answered between 19:00 and 01:00, 20 were picked up by Michelle and Rutendo after their shift ended. The overflow team answered 16 of the 143 calls in that window (11.2%). After 21:00, when Michelle and Rutendo stop picking up, the answer rate falls to 14.1% (13 of 92).

Opportunity: weekend coverage

The team does not work weekends, so every weekend caller goes unanswered. This year 13 calls arrived over the weekend, after the Friday overflow shift ended at 01:00 CAT. 3 were spam, leaving 10 genuine callers who reached nobody: 9 went to voicemail and 1 hung up.

10genuine weekend calls, 0 answered
8 of 10arrived on a Saturday, US Central time; the other 2 on a Sunday

Those callers are likely to go elsewhere: 82% of US consumers say they will call a competitor when a business does not answer (CallRail 2025, via Missed Call Statistics 2026). A short Saturday shift, or a missed-call text-back on weekends, would capture them.

About 3 in 10 calls are spam

Spam falls into two groups: calls Zoom flagged itself, and calls that were answered but lasted 5 seconds or less, which usually means a robocall, a silent line or a misdial.
